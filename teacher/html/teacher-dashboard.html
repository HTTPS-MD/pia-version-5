Teacher Help Center

01What this page is for

This is your section’s student directory. It shows who is in your section, who is offline right now, and who has entered the study today.

It is read-only. You can look, but you cannot change anything about a student or the study.

02Reading the directory
  • Online: the student is signed in and active now. The dot beside the name is green when online.
  • Participation: “Entered” means the student has entered the study. “Not in yet” means they have not. It is greyed out while the student is offline.
  • Study has started / not yet started: shows whether the administrator has started the session for your section.
  • Privacy Mode: the switch at the top hides the students’ names on your screen (only their initials show), for example when the screen is projected. It is off at first and only changes your own screen.
  • All Students / Offline tabs and Search: show everyone or only those who are offline, or find a student by name.

The list refreshes by itself every few seconds, so you do not need to reload it.

03How the class did, by topic

After the administrator closes the Game for your section, three boxes appear at the top of the page, one for each topic: Finding a percentage, Percentage increase and Percentage decrease.

  • The number is the class accuracy in that topic: how many of the students’ answers were correct, on average. 100% means every answer was correct; 60% means about 6 of every 10 answers were.
  • It is the class only. It never says who. No name, score or answer is shown. A dash means nobody answered in that topic.
  • Before the Game is closed the boxes are not shown, so the study is not influenced while students are working. If the Game is opened again, they disappear until it is closed.
  • How it is counted: for each student, correct answers divided by all their answers in the topic (a retry after a wrong answer counts as another answer); then the average of the students who answered. Every student counts equally.
04Changing your section

If the administrator gave you more than one section, the section name at the top of the page becomes a menu. Choose another section to switch to it.

If you see “You are not assigned to a section yet,” ask the administrator to assign you one.

05What you cannot see, and why

To protect students, this page does not show personality results, assigned tutor, scores, or any individual performance data. Those are kept for the research team only, as agreed in the study’s ethical review. The only performance information you get is the class accuracy by topic, after the Game is closed.

If you notice a problem with a student’s record, tell the administrator.

06Your account
  • Settings: open it from the account menu at the top right to see your details and change your password.
  • Password: changing it signs your other devices out. This one stays signed in.
  • Light or dark theme: use the sun or moon button beside your account.
  • Sign Out: also in the account menu. You are signed out automatically after a period of inactivity.
