Teacher Help Center

01What this page is for

This is your section’s student directory. It shows who is in your section, who is online right now, and today’s attendance.

It is read-only. You can look, but you cannot change anything about a student or the study.

02Reading the directory
  • Online: the student is signed in and active now. The dot beside the name is green when online.
  • Attendance: Present or Absent as marked by the administrator. “Not marked” means the roll has not been called for that student yet.
  • Class is open / Study is not yet started: shows whether attendance has been opened for your section.
  • All / Online tabs and Search: show everyone or only those online, or find a student by name.

The list refreshes by itself every few seconds, so you do not need to reload it.

03Changing your section

If the administrator gave you more than one section, the section name at the top of the page becomes a menu. Choose another section to switch to it.

If you see “You are not assigned to a section yet,” ask the administrator to assign you one.

04What you cannot see, and why

To protect students, this page does not show personality results, assigned tutor, scores, or any individual performance data. Those are kept for the research team only, as agreed in the study’s ethical review.

If you notice a problem with a student’s record, tell the administrator.

05Your account
  • Settings: open it from the account menu at the top right to see your details and change your password.
  • Password: changing it signs your other devices out. This one stays signed in.
  • Light or dark theme: use the sun or moon button beside your account.
  • Sign Out: also in the account menu. You are signed out automatically after a period of inactivity.
06Still need help?

If something here does not answer your question, or something on the page looks wrong, email the PIA team. Tell us your section and what you were doing.
