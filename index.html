Got a big problem? Cut it into panels!

Here is one the tutors solve with you. A ₱100 bag now costs ₱120. By what percent did the price jump?

  1. The problem

    ₱100 →to ₱120

    PIA: The price went up. By what percent? Let’s find out.

  2. Find the change

    ₱120 − ₱100 ₱20 more

    PIA: First, how much did it go up? New price minus old price.

  3. Compare to the start

    20 ÷ 120 100 = 0.20

    PIA: Divide by the ORIGINAL price, where it started. That’s the whole trick.

  4. Done ✓

    20%

    Check: 20% of ₱100 = ₱20 ✓
