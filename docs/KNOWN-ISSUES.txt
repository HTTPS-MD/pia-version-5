KNOWN ISSUES AND TO-DO (Version 05, staging)
Written 2026-10-26. Nothing here has been changed in code yet.

1. Opening a stage only lets in the students who are ready at that moment
   - Turning on "Tutoring dashboard" (or the other stage switches) runs admin_grant_stage once.
     It moves the students who qualify right then. Students who are not ready yet are skipped
     and are NOT picked up later. They stay in the Waiting Room, and refreshing does not help.
   - Seen on staging: Test Student Two (Free choice, Ava chosen) and Test Student Three (Neutral)
     stayed in the Waiting Room after the Tutoring switch was on.
     Switching Tutoring OFF and ON again let both in.
   - The cause for Test Student Three is NOT confirmed. The "no character chosen yet" reason
     only explains Test Student Two. Timing (switch turned on before they finished) is a guess.
   - Not tested on dev. The code is the same copy (admin_grant_stage, waiting-room.js),
     so dev probably behaves the same, but this is not proven.
   - Why it matters: in a real class students finish at different speeds.
   - Workaround for now: open Tutoring only after everyone is done, or switch OFF and ON again.
   - Possible fix (to decide and test, never on dev): when the stage is open for the section,
     let any qualifying student in when they reach the Waiting Room or when they refresh.
