KNOWN ISSUES AND TO-DO (Version 05, staging)
Written 2026-10-26. Nothing here has been changed in code yet.

1. Opening a stage only lets in the students who are ready at that moment
   - Turning on "Tutoring dashboard" (or the other stage switches) runs admin_grant_stage once.
     It moves the students who qualify right then. Students who are not ready yet are skipped
     and are NOT picked up later. They stay in the Waiting Room, and refreshing does not help.
   - Seen on staging: Test Student Two (Free choice, Ava chosen) and Test Student Three (Neutral)
     stayed in the Waiting Room after the Tutoring switch was on.
     Switching Tutoring OFF and ON again let both in.
   - Cause for Test Student Three, retested on 2026-10-26: it was timing. With Three already in the
     Waiting Room (OCEAN done) and Tutoring OFF, one click on ON sent Three straight into tutoring.
     So the grant works for students who are ready at that moment, and skips the others for good.
     (Test Student Two was skipped because no character was chosen yet at that time.)
   - Not tested on dev. The code is the same copy (admin_grant_stage, waiting-room.js),
     so dev probably behaves the same, but this is not proven.
   - Why it matters: in a real class students finish at different speeds.
   - Workaround for now: open Tutoring only after everyone is done, or switch OFF and ON again.
   - Possible fix (to decide and test, never on dev): when the stage is open for the section,
     let any qualifying student in when they reach the Waiting Room or when they refresh.

   PROPOSED SOLUTION for item 1 (not done yet; to be tested on staging first)

           admin_grant_stage returns, so the admin can see who was left out.

           Waiting Room, check that record for their section and send them in. Today only the
           global setting is checked, which stays false for a section-only opening.

           (the same check in set_student_stage on the database side).
   Step 3. Listen live. The Waiting Room should also listen for changes to the section record,
           so a student who finishes later moves on by itself without a refresh.
   Step 4. Test on staging with three students who finish at different times
           (before, during and after the switch is turned on). All three must get in.
   Step 5. Rerun the behavior tests in ~/pia-backups/behavior-tests so nothing else changes.
   Until this is fixed: open Tutoring only after everyone is done, or switch OFF and ON again.
   Rule: change Version 05 only. Never change dev or the real database for this.
