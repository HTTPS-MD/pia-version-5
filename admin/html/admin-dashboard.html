Admin Help Center

01How the study runs

Each student goes through three stages in order: the OCEAN test (a short personality questionnaire), Choose tutor, and the Game (math problems with the tutor).

Students have no password. They enter with their student number, and only after you mark them present and open the stage for their section.

02Sections and students
  • Manage sections / New section: create or rename a section, or delete one that has no students.
  • Add Students: add students to the chosen section. Each needs a name and an 11-digit student number.
  • Student table: shows each student’s section, condition (study group), stage, and character. Use the stage tabs and Reset filters to narrow the list.
  • Student drawer: click a student to see details, move them to another section, or fix their record.
  • Input Scores: enter the paper pre-test and post-test scores for the section.
03Roll call (attendance)

Mark each student Present or Absent for the session. A student who is not marked present cannot enter. Teachers see this attendance read-only.

04Opening and closing stages

Use the switch beside each stage tab to open or close that stage for the whole section. A student who opens a page for a stage that is not open to them is sent back to the page they belong on.

The stage tabs list students by where they are right now. The Offline tab lists students who are not signed in.

05Teachers
  • Add teacher: creates the account and assigns the teacher to a section.
  • Teacher profile: shows the teacher’s section and account status. From here you can remove the teacher from the section, reset their password, sign them out everywhere, or remove the account. To give a section a teacher again, use Assign teacher on the section.
  • A teacher sees only the student directory of their own section, read-only. They never see personality results.
06Math Task (question bank)

Questions here are used to build the game problems. Add or edit a question, review its steps and answers, and save. Use Rules to adjust the adaptive rules for the problems.

Check every step and answer before students play, because the tutor teaches from them.

07Personality and tutor assignment

The OCEAN scores (Extraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Neuroticism, Openness) are used only for students in the Assigned group, whose tutor is matched to their result. In Free choice the student picks the tutor, Neutral gets a neutral tutor, and Control has a traditional lesson with no tutor.

Personality results are confidential. They are for the research team only and are never shown to teachers or students.

08Your account and other admins
  • Settings: your details, change password, and the list of administrators. Use Add administrator to invite another.
  • Password: changing it signs your other devices out.
  • Light or dark theme: in the account menu or the top bar.
  • Delete account: removes your own administrator account for good. You cannot delete another administrator, and the last administrator cannot be deleted.

Scoring rules All topics

A question's score = its maximum points, minus a penalty for each wrong answer and each hint, plus a bonus for a fast answer with no wrong answer. Percentages are of that question's own maximum, so they work for any point value. The server works the score out when the question is finished; students never see it.
